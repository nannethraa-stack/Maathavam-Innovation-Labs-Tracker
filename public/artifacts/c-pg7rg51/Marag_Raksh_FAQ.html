Marag Raksh – Frequently Asked Questions

Real-World Impact & Criticality Analysis for Strategic Review

Q1: What is Marag Raksh?

Concept: Marag Raksh is a non-intrusive, AI-powered vehicle screening system that continuously checks every vehicle for weight anomalies, concealed compartments, and thermal irregularities — without stopping traffic.
Impact & Criticality: Today, less than 5% of vehicles on Indian highways and borders are screened. This leaves the country exposed to large-scale gold and narcotics smuggling while costing an estimated ~3% of GDP annually through unsafe and unmonitored roads. Marag Raksh closes that gap at national scale.

Q2: How does it actually work?

Concept: Piezoelectric sensors in the road measure real-time weight, infrared cameras detect hidden heat signatures, and AI vision identifies the vehicle and occupancy. These three streams are fused into a single Low/Moderate/High risk score sent instantly to interception teams while vehicles move at 20–80 km/h.
Impact & Criticality: Manual checks and random inspections are easily bypassed and create massive congestion. This system delivers continuous, automated intelligence without adding a single second of delay to traffic flow, turning every corridor into a live security layer.

Q3: What makes the technology genuinely novel?

Concept: No deployed system anywhere combines weight deviation + thermal anomaly + visual classification into one composite risk score for non-stop, road-embedded screening. This multi-sensor fusion is the core inventive step.
Impact & Criticality: Existing tools each solve only a fraction of the problem. Without fusion, authorities are forced to choose between coverage, accuracy, or traffic flow. Marag Raksh removes that trade-off, giving security forces a decisive new capability that simply does not exist today.

Q4: How does it outperform current systems?

Concept: It uniquely combines zero traffic stoppage, weight anomaly detection, thermal detection, AI identification, composite risk scoring, radiation-free scalability, 100% coverage, and all-weather operation.
Impact & Criticality: X-ray systems cost over USD 1 million per unit, disrupt traffic, and cannot be rolled out nationally. Manual checks cover almost nothing. Standard weigh-in-motion systems are used only for overload enforcement. Marag Raksh is the first practical answer deployable at corridor scale.

Q5: Why is this urgent for India right now? (Atmanirbhar Bharat Vision)

Concept & Context: India continues to face large-scale gold smuggling, multi-tonne narcotics seizures, and an estimated ~3% annual GDP drag from unsafe and largely unscreened roads. Every day of delay leaves critical corridors open to organised crime.
Impact & Criticality: Aligned with Viksit Bharat 2047, Marag Raksh is a pure expression of indigenous innovation, solving India's unique scale problem without radiation or traffic paralysis. Fast-tracking it builds domestic capability, reduces import dependence, and protects national assets.

Q6: Will it slow down traffic or logistics?

Concept: No. Vehicles pass at normal highway speeds, and only high-risk vehicles are intercepted downstream.
Impact & Criticality: Traditional checkpoints create bottlenecks and impose daily economic costs on freight. Marag Raksh eliminates that friction entirely, protecting security without taxing the economy or public patience.

Q7: Can it work reliably in Indian conditions (rain, fog, night, mixed traffic)?

Concept: Yes. The infrared module operates in zero light, fog, and rain, while weight sensors function across the typical speed band with AI continuously cross-checking data.
Impact & Criticality: Security systems that fail in monsoons or at night create predictable windows of vulnerability. Marag Raksh removes those windows, delivering consistent protection 24x7.

Q8: Is it scalable and cost-effective compared with X-ray solutions?

Concept: Yes. It uses road-embedded sensors and cameras, requires no radiation shielding or dedicated lanes, and integrates directly into existing toll plazas.
Impact & Criticality: High-cost, low-coverage X-ray installations can never achieve national reach. Marag Raksh allows rapid, widespread deployment at a fraction of the disruption and cost.

Q9: Where should it be deployed first for maximum effect?

Concept: National/state highways, toll plazas, urban entry points, border checkposts, and industrial/port perimeters.
Impact & Criticality: These are the highest-volume, highest-risk corridors. Placing the system here immediately multiplies detection capacity across routes carrying the bulk of security threats.

Q10: What is the strategic and market opportunity?

Concept: The global cargo and vehicle screening market is projected to reach $4.04 billion by 2030, with rapid growth in weigh-in-motion and thermal imaging sectors.
Impact & Criticality: First-mover advantage belongs to those who act now. Fast-tracking Marag Raksh secures both national capability and global leadership in a high-growth security technology domain.
